This calendar is shared — everyone sees and edits the same schedule, which lives in the database and persists across deploys. Each Mon–Fri is auto-assigned a single ★ primary for every shift it runs (configurable — one shift or two), with the workload balanced evenly across all shifts combined. There's no backup slot. A green day has every shift covered, yellow means one is still open, red means none are. Click a weekday to toggle whether you can't cover it — that marks you off for the whole day and the month (today onward) reshuffles to fill the gap. Right-click or long-press a day for finer control: when two shifts run you can mark yourself off a single shift; either way you can lock yourself into a shift as primary — locked claims survive reshuffles (shown with a lock icon). No one is put on two shifts of the same day, and no one leads the same shift two weekdays running (unless it's the only way to fill it). Weekends aren't auto-assigned. Saturdays are an open sign-up — click one to add the hours you can work; any number of people can sign up and overlapping hours are fine. Sundays are no-coverage by default — click one to claim it if something comes up. Right-click a weekday or Saturday and choose No coverage needed to grey it out for holidays or shutdowns — it's skipped by balancing until you restore it. Past days stay frozen as a record. Backup downloads a full snapshot and Restore brings one back. Coverage rules lets you guarantee an engineer a set number of ★ days each week (spread out, back-to-back allowed only for them, never on shifts they've marked off).
